Introduction to Science

Science is the systematic study of natural world. It helps us understand how things work and allows us to develope technologies
that improce our daily lives. Scientific knowlkedge is based on observation, evidence, experimentation, and careful analysis.

The Solar System consist of the Sun and the object that orbit it, including planets, moons, asteroids, and comets. Earth is the third
planet from the Sun and is the only known planet that supports life. The Earth contains approximately 71% water. Water is represented
chemically as H20, While carbon Dioxide is represented as CO2.
