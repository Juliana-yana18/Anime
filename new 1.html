DISCOVER



The history of anime began in the 1910s with early animation experiments, eventually leading to Japan's first color feature film by Toei Animation in 1958.
The industry experienced a massive revolution in 1963 when Osamu Tezuka's Astro Boy established the stylistic blueprint for modern television series.
Throughout the 1970s and 1980s, anime expanded rapidly with the birth of iconic genres like mecha and the founding of legendary studios like Studio Ghibli.
Today, driven by digital production advances and global streaming platforms, anime has evolved from a regional art form into a massive, multi-billion-dollar global phenomenon.
